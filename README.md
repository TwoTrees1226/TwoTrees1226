# HY Wei

I build software around problems I know firsthand.

My background is in law. I spent six years at Google working across legal, commercial, and product-related matters. Today, I’m building Two Trees, turning legal and operational complexity into useful software.

## What I’m building

[LAWSKEY](https://lawskey.com/) is a contract management and obligation-tracking platform, connecting contract creation and review with the work that follows signing.

The question behind it is simple: how do we turn what an agreement says into commitments people can actually track and follow through on?

My work spans product design, legal workflows, customer discovery, and collaboration with engineering.

## What interests me

- Turning professional knowledge into usable tools
- Connecting documents, obligations, and decisions
- Understanding how systems shape the way people work
